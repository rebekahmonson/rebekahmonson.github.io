Session 3 · Strategy · Oct 20

HW · Case packet + skeptical stakeholder
Read the case, vote scale-or-kill in the app. Then AI plays a cranky stakeholder attacking your own product's assumptions.
→ position vote + scariest assumption + kill criteria
→
11:00 · Callback + poll
Build callback, then scariest assumptions on screen.
→
11:10 · Lecture
Strategy, metrics, prioritization: focus is the scarce resource.
→
12:00 · Case clinic
Mixed teams (scale + kill voters) produce a recommendation + kill criteria.
→
12:35 · Full group + reveal
Teams argue, cohort votes, then what actually happened. Synthesis back to your product.
